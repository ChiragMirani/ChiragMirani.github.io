Upanishads English translation and meaning

What are the Upanishads?

The Upanishads are Sanskrit philosophical texts of the Vedic tradition focused on Atman, Brahman, knowledge, meditation, and liberation.

Which translations are used?

The English text is sourced from Max Müller's public-domain Sacred Books of the East translations, with direct source links on every section.

Does every section have meaning?

Each section includes the translation, a plain meaning generated from the English, a key idea, and a study relevance note.

Does it include Sanskrit?

Aligned Sanskrit and generated transliteration are shown where a source was attached safely. Other sections are marked source-needed.
