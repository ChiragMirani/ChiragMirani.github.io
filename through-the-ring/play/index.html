How to play

Tap the screen, click the mouse or press the Space bar (or the up arrow) to make the ball fly up. Let go and it falls. Fly through each golden ring and the wall behind it slides open so you can pass. Touch a wall, the spiky floor or the spiky ceiling and you lose a life. You have three lives, and every ring you pass speeds the game up a little.

How scoring works

  • Edge 1 point, Good 2, Perfect 3, Bullseye 5 (right through the middle).
  • Streaks: 3 Perfect passes in a row double your points, 8 triple them, 16 quadruple them.
  • Special rings: Gold rings are worth x2, Prism rings x3 and Angel rings x10.
  • Power Flight: a Bullseye can launch three guided rings at triple points.
  • Life rings: every 25th ring gives back a lost life.

What games are like Flappy Tappy?

Flappy Tappy is a one-tap flying game in the family of classic tap-to-flap arcade games, with a twist: instead of only dodging, you aim for the middle of each golden ring, and streaks multiply your score.
