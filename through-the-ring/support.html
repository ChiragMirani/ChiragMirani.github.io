Questions People Ask

How do I get my purchase back on a new phone?

Through the Ring PRO is paid once, up front, so there is nothing to restore inside the game. Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, then Purchased (or Purchases), find the game, and tap the cloud icon to download it again. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy inside the game. You pay once, when you download it.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. The game works with no internet, including in airplane mode. Game Center leaderboards and friend challenges are optional and need a connection.

How does today's leaderboard work?

Every Play run counts on two Game Center leaderboards: today's board, which resets once a day (the game shows your local reset time), and the all-time board. There is no separate Daily Ring mode anymore. After each run you see your place, like “You're #4 of 11 today.” Slow Mode and Practice runs are not scored online.

How do I challenge a friend?

After a normal Play run, tap Challenge a friend on the game-over card. It opens the share sheet with your score card, a line like “I scored 1,240 on Through the Ring. Can you beat it?” and the App Store link, so you can send it by Messages, WhatsApp or anywhere. Your friend does not need Game Center. On iOS 26 you can also start a Game Center challenge, Beat My Best Run, from Apple's Games app.

What languages is the game in?

Nine: English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian and Traditional Chinese. The game follows your device language. To choose a different language for just this game, open the iOS Settings app, find Through the Ring PRO, and tap Language.

Will my best score move to a new phone?

Your in-game best score is saved only on the device you play on. A new phone starts at zero, and so does reinstalling the app. If you use Game Center, your leaderboard scores are kept by Apple with your Game Center account, so they still appear on the leaderboards from a new phone.

Which devices and iOS versions does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo with iOS 17 or later, in portrait. On iPhone Duo you can play on either display. Game Center challenges in Apple's Games app need iOS 26 or later; Challenge a friend by message works on every supported version. Version 2.0 is a full rebuild, so scores from the old version do not carry over.

How do I turn off the sound or the vibration?

Open Settings in the game for separate Music, Sound effects and Vibration controls. During a run, the speaker button next to pause mutes or unmutes sound. The game remembers your choice. The game also respects your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting.
