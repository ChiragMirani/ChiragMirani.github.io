Get the Game Back

Flappy Tappy is free to download. To get it again, open the App Store, tap your profile picture, tap Purchased, and download it from your list, using the same Apple Account. If you bought Unlock the Full Game, or bought the game before it became free, open Settings → Full Game in the game and tap Restore purchase. Everything you paid for stays unlocked.

Questions People Ask

How do I get my purchase back on a new phone?

Download Flappy Tappy again from the App Store with the same Apple Account (it is free to download), then open Settings → Full Game in the game and tap Restore purchase. This works whether you bought Unlock the Full Game or bought the game before it became free.

Does the game have ads, a subscription or in-app purchases?

There are no ads and no subscription. The download is free and includes 3 free scored runs, available immediately, with three starting lives per run. There is one optional purchase, Unlock the Full Game, a one-time $2.99 in the US (prices vary by country) for unlimited runs.

I bought Flappy Tappy before. Do I have to pay again?

No. If you bought the game before it became free, everything stays unlocked and you do not pay again. If it ever shows as locked, for example on a new device, open Settings → Full Game in the game and tap Restore purchase.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. The game works with no internet, including in airplane mode. Game Center leaderboards and friend challenges are optional and need a connection.

How does today's leaderboard work?

Every Play run counts on two Game Center leaderboards: today's board, which resets once a day (the game shows your local reset time), and the all-time board. There is no separate Daily Ring mode anymore. After each run you see your place, like “You're #4 of 11 today.” Slow Mode runs are not scored online.

How do I challenge a friend?

After a normal Play run, tap Challenge a friend on the game-over card. It opens the share sheet with your score card, a line like “I scored 1,240 on Flappy Tappy. Can you beat it?” and the App Store link, so you can send it by Messages, WhatsApp or anywhere. Your friend does not need Game Center. On iOS 26 you can also start a Game Center challenge, Beat My Best Run, from Apple's Games app.

What languages is the game in?

Nine: English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian and Traditional Chinese. The game follows your device language. To choose a different language for just this game, open the iOS Settings app, find Flappy Tappy, and tap Language.

Will my best score move to a new phone?

Your in-game best score is saved only on the device you play on. A new phone starts at zero, and so does reinstalling the app. If you use Game Center, your leaderboard scores are kept by Apple with your Game Center account, so they still appear on the leaderboards from a new phone.

Which devices and iOS versions does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo with iOS 17 or later, in portrait. On iPhone Duo you can play on either display. Game Center challenges in Apple's Games app need iOS 26 or later; Challenge a friend by message works on every supported version. Version 2.0 is a full rebuild, so scores from the old version do not carry over.

How do I turn off the sound or the vibration?

Open Settings in the game for separate Music, Sound effects and Vibration controls. During a run, the speaker button next to pause mutes or unmutes sound. The game remembers your choice. The game also respects your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting.
