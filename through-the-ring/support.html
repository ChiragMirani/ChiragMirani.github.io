Flappy Tappy support: Solo, duels and purchases

Flappy Tappy is free to download. Endless Solo and friend duels are free. Other duels include a free allowance, with an optional one-time purchase for unlimited duels.

Why does a duel need the internet?

Live duels connect two players. Check your connection and invite your friend again. Offline play is available in Solo.

How do I play with a friend?

Send the friend-duel link from the app. During a FaceTime call, choose Duel on FaceTime. Both players need a compatible installation and connection.

Purchases and earlier buyers

Use Settings → Full Game to restore the existing unlock with the Apple account that purchased it. The privacy policy describes Restore in Shop for cosmetic balls in versions that offer them. Apple manages payment and refund requests.

Report a problem

Email Flappy Tappy support with your app version, device, iOS version and steps to reproduce. Do not send payment details or passwords.
