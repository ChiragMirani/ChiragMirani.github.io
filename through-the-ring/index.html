Through the Ring PRO

Tap to jump. Fly through the ring. Open the wall.

See It on the App Store

What the Game Is

A glowing ball rolls through a never-ending course. Tap anywhere on the screen and it jumps; let go and gravity brings it back down. Each ring you fly through opens the wall just behind it. Miss the ring and the wall stays shut. A closed wall ends your run, and so do the spikes on the floor and the ceiling.

You score one point for every ring. Then you try to beat your best.

How It Treats You

  • No ads. Not one, not ever.
  • No tracking and no account. The game collects nothing about you.
  • No internet needed. It works on a plane.
  • Sound and vibration switches on the menu, and it works with VoiceOver.

Made For

iPhone and iPad, in portrait. It needs iOS 17 or later. One price, paid once, no purchases inside.

Coming in version 2.1: compatible with iPhone Duo. Play in portrait on either display. Both displays, large text, and paused-game folding and unfolding have been checked in the iPhone Duo simulator. Version 2.1 is submitted to Apple for review.
