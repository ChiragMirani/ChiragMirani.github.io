Flappy Tappy: ad-free casual game, Solo and live duels

A casual game for quick breaks: tap to fly through rings on your own, or challenge friends to live duels of up to 30 seconds. No ads and no subscription.

Where Flappy Tappy fits best

Looking for your next favorite casual game? Flappy Tappy is built for short, one-tap sessions and friendly competition. Endless Solo and duels with friends are free. Play a quick round, send a friend a link and try a rematch.

Solo, friend duels and FaceTime

  • Solo: endless offline play with three lives per run.
  • Friend duels: live rounds of up to 30 seconds, free for both players.
  • FaceTime: choose Duel on FaceTime to play while you talk.
  • Other duels: the first 100 are free, then five per day. An optional one-time purchase adds unlimited duels.

Online duels and Game Center features need an internet connection. Offline play applies to Solo.

Try the browser preview

Play a free taste of Flappy Tappy in your browser. Tap, click or press Space to fly. The browser preview is a Solo experience; live duels and FaceTime play are features of the iPhone and iPad app.

Inside the game

Screens show Solo gameplay. Available features depend on the installed version.

How to play

Tap to fly up and release to fall. Aim through the rings to open each gate. Center passes earn more points, perfect streaks multiply your score, and a bullseye can activate Power Flight. In duels, the first player to fall loses; if both survive, the higher score wins.

Privacy and purchases

No separate Flappy Tappy account is required. The privacy policy explains version-specific data handling, including optional analytics announced for version 2.5.3 and later.
