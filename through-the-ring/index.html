Flappy Tappy: Ad-Free One-Tap Game with FaceTime SharePlay

Flappy Tappy is an ad-free one-tap game for iPhone and iPad. Play free endless Solo offline, challenge a friend to a live duel of up to 30 seconds, or play together during a FaceTime call using SharePlay. No subscription.

Get Flappy Tappy on the App Store

Where Flappy Tappy fits best

Looking for your next favorite casual game? Flappy Tappy is built for short, one-tap sessions and friendly competition. Endless Solo and duels with friends are free. Play a quick round, send a friend a link and try a rematch.

Solo, friend duels and FaceTime

  • Solo: endless offline play with three lives per run.
  • Friend duels: live rounds of up to 30 seconds, free for both players.
  • FaceTime SharePlay: choose Duel on FaceTime to play a live one-tap duel while you talk.
  • Other duels: the first 100 are free, then five per day. An optional one-time purchase adds unlimited duels.

Online duels and Game Center features need an internet connection. Offline play applies to Solo.

Try the browser preview

Play a free taste of Flappy Tappy in your browser. Tap, click or press Space to fly. The browser preview is a Solo experience; live duels and FaceTime play are features of the iPhone and iPad app.

Does Flappy Tappy support FaceTime SharePlay?

Yes. The iPhone and iPad app supports SharePlay for live friend duels during FaceTime calls. Both players need the app and an internet connection. The official App Store listing confirms SharePlay support under Supports.

How to play Flappy Tappy with a friend on FaceTime. The browser teaser is Solo only and does not support SharePlay.

How to play

Tap to fly up and release to fall. Aim through the rings to open each gate. Center passes earn more points, perfect streaks multiply your score, and a bullseye can activate Power Flight. In duels, the first player to fall loses; if both survive, the higher score wins.

Privacy and purchases

No separate Flappy Tappy account is required. The privacy policy explains version-specific data handling, including optional analytics announced for version 2.5.3 and later. See the policy and current App Store privacy label before installing. Purchase options and local prices appear in the App Store and in the app.
