What the Game Is

A glowing ball rolls through a never-ending course. Tap anywhere on the screen and it jumps; let go and gravity brings it back down. Each ring you fly through opens the wall just behind it. Miss the ring and the wall stays shut. A closed wall ends your run, and so do the spikes on the floor and the ceiling.

Aim for the center of each ring to earn more points, and watch for special rings worth 2x, 3x and 10x. Then you try to beat your best.

Today's Leaderboard

Every Play run counts on two Game Center leaderboards: today's board, which resets once a day, and the all-time board. After each run you see your place, like “You're #4 of 11 today.”

Think you can do better than a friend? Send them a Game Center challenge: Beat My Best Run (iOS 26 or later). Game Center is optional. Without it, the game plays exactly the same.

How It Treats You

  • No ads. Not one, not ever.
  • No tracking and no account needed. The game collects nothing about you.
  • No internet needed. It works on a plane. Game Center leaderboards and challenges are optional and need a connection.
  • Music, sound and vibration controls in Settings, and it works with VoiceOver.
  • Now in 9 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian and Traditional Chinese.

Made For

iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, in portrait (on either iPhone Duo display). It needs iOS 17 or later. One price, paid once, no purchases inside.
