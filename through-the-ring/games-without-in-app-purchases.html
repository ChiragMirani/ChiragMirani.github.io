iPhone Games Without In-App Purchases

To find iPhone games without in-app purchases, check the App Store page: if it does not say “In-App Purchases” next to the price button or in the Information section, there are none. One game that qualifies is Through the Ring PRO, a $2.99 one-tap arcade game for iPhone and iPad that you pay for once, with no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy inside.

How to Check Any Game in 30 Seconds

  1. Open the game's App Store page. Next to the Get or price button, look for the small “In-App Purchases” label. No label means nothing is sold inside.
  2. Scroll to Information. If there is an In-App Purchases row, tap it to see every item and its price.
  3. Read the App Privacy section. It shows whether the developer collects data, which is common in ad-funded games.
  4. Look for words like “pay once” or “premium” in the description, then confirm with steps 1 and 2. The labels are what count.

Why Through the Ring PRO Fits

  • One price, $2.99 (US), paid once. You get the complete game.
  • Nothing for sale inside: no coins, no extra lives, no unlocks, no subscription. Lives, Power Flight, secrets and ball colors are earned by playing.
  • No ads and no analytics or tracking.
  • No account needed, and no internet needed to play.

Honest Limits

  • It is one small arcade game, not a big adventure. If you want story or levels, it will not be enough.
  • There is no free trial. Watch the preview video on the App Store page before you buy.
  • It runs on iPhone and iPad only (iOS 17 or later), in portrait.

Questions People Ask

How can I tell if an iPhone game has in-app purchases?

On the game's App Store page, look next to the Get or price button: games with purchases inside say “In-App Purchases.” Scroll to the Information section too, where an In-App Purchases row lists what is for sale. If neither appears, there are none.

Does Through the Ring PRO have any in-app purchases?

No. It costs $2.99 once in the US App Store, and nothing inside the game is for sale: no coins, no lives, no unlocks and no subscription.

Are paid games always free of in-app purchases?

No. Some paid games still sell extras inside. Check the App Store page every time, even when a game has a price.

Can I just turn off in-app purchases instead?

Yes. Screen Time can block in-app purchases: open Settings, Screen Time, Content & Privacy Restrictions, then the App Store purchases setting, and set In-app Purchases to Don't Allow. That blocks buying, but a game may still show offers.

Does Through the Ring PRO have ads?

No. There are no ads of any kind, and the game has no ad network or analytics inside.
