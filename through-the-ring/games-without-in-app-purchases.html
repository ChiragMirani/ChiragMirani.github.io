iPhone Games With No Ads and No Subscription

To find iPhone games with no ads and no subscription, check the App Store page: the In-App Purchases row under Information lists everything for sale, and App Privacy shows whether a game collects data for advertising. One example is Through the Ring PRO, a one-tap arcade game for iPhone and iPad with no ads and no subscription. To be clear, it is not a game without in-app purchases: it is free to try, with one optional one-time unlock.

How to Check Any Game in 30 Seconds

  1. Open the game's App Store page. Next to the Get or price button, look for the small “In-App Purchases” label. No label means nothing is sold inside.
  2. Scroll to Information. If there is an In-App Purchases row, tap it to see every item and its price. Monthly or yearly items are subscriptions; a single “unlock” or “full game” item is usually a one-time purchase.
  3. Read the App Privacy section. Data used for third-party advertising or tracking is common in ad-funded games.
  4. Test it offline. Play a round in airplane mode and again online. Ads that only appear online are easy to spot this way.

Questions People Ask

How can I tell if an iPhone game has ads?

The App Store has no ads label, so check two places. In App Privacy, ad-funded games usually list data used for third-party advertising or tracking. Then play a few rounds in airplane mode and again online: ads that appear only online are a sure sign.

How can I tell if an iPhone game has in-app purchases or a subscription?

On the game's App Store page, look next to the Get or price button: games with purchases inside say “In-App Purchases.” Scroll to the Information section, where an In-App Purchases row lists each item and its price. Items that renew, such as monthly or yearly passes, are subscriptions.

Does Through the Ring PRO have ads or a subscription?

No. There are no ads of any kind and no subscription, and the game has no ad network or analytics inside.

Does Through the Ring PRO have in-app purchases?

Yes, exactly one, and it is optional: Unlock the Full Game, a one-time $2.99 in the US (prices vary by country) for unlimited runs. The download is free and includes 3 free runs, at most one per day, and Practice is always free. People who bought the game before keep everything unlocked.

Can I turn off in-app purchases on a child's iPhone?

Yes. Screen Time can block them: open Settings, Screen Time, Content & Privacy Restrictions, then the App Store purchases setting, and set In-app Purchases to Don't Allow. With Family Sharing, Ask to Buy sends each purchase request to a parent instead.
