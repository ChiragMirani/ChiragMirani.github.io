Play Flappy Tappy on FaceTime with SharePlay

Flappy Tappy is an ad-free one-tap iPhone and iPad game with live friend duels during FaceTime calls. SharePlay lets you talk and play at the same time, with each player on their own device. Friend duels are free for both players.

Get Flappy Tappy on the App Store

What you need

  • Flappy Tappy installed on both devices, with compatible iOS or iPadOS versions. The App Store lists iOS 17 or later and iPadOS 17 or later.
  • An active FaceTime call and an internet connection for both players.
  • A friend who has the app and accepts the invitation.

How to start a FaceTime duel

  1. Start a FaceTime call with your friend.
  2. Open Flappy Tappy while the call continues.
  3. Tap Duel on FaceTime and follow the SharePlay invitation prompts.
  4. Have your friend join, then tap to fly through the rings. Each duel lasts up to 30 seconds: the first player to fall loses; if both survive, the higher score wins.

The button wording comes from the developer's App Store description. The same listing confirms SharePlay under Supports. Interface details may change with app or operating-system updates.

Are there ads or subscriptions?

No ads or subscription. Solo and friend duels are always free. Matchmaking against other players has a free allowance; an optional one-time purchase adds unlimited duels. Optional cosmetic purchases are also available. Check the current App Store listing for local prices and purchase options.

If your friend cannot join

Check that both devices have internet access, compatible installed versions and an active FaceTime call. Reopen the app and try the invitation again. A regular friend-duel link is another way to play online. For help, see Flappy Tappy support; include your device, operating-system version and app version when reporting a problem.
