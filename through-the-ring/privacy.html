Privacy

Through the Ring PRO collects nothing about you. Here is the whole story.

Last updated 6 October 2026

What We Collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet to play, and it has no one to send your data to.

What Stays on Your Device

The game remembers three things so it can greet you properly next time: your best score, whether sound is on, and whether vibration is on. They are saved on your iPhone or iPad only, in the app's own settings storage (called UserDefaults). They never leave your device. Delete the app and they are gone.

Share and Rate Buttons

If you tap Share, the standard iOS share sheet opens with a short message and the App Store link, and you choose where it goes. If you tap Rate, the App Store opens on the game's page. Those happen only when you tap, and they happen in Apple's apps, under Apple's privacy policy.

Apple and the App Store

Apple knows you bought the game, the way it knows about every purchase. We do not receive your name, your email or your purchase details from Apple. The game does not use Game Center.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, there is nothing about children to collect either.

If This Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top.
