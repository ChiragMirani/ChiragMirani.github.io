Privacy

Flappy Tappy collects nothing about you. Here is the whole story.

Last updated Sunday, October 18, 2026

What We Collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not need the internet to play, and we run no servers to send your data to.

What Stays on Your Device

The game remembers a few things so it can greet you properly next time: your best scores, your settings (such as music, sound and vibration), the ball you picked, the secrets you have found, an unfinished run you can continue, how many of your free runs you have used, and whether the full game is unlocked (Apple confirms the purchase; we never see your payment details). They are saved on your iPhone or iPad only, in the app's own settings storage (called UserDefaults). We never receive them. Delete the app and they are gone. If you turn on reminders, iOS schedules them on your device; there is no reminder server.

Game Center (Optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the score from each Play run goes to Apple's Game Center so it can appear on today's leaderboard and the all-time leaderboard, and show your place after the run. Other players may see your Game Center nickname and score, according to your Game Center settings and Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your name, your email or any other details from Game Center. Slow Mode runs are not sent. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent and the game plays the same.

Challenge a friend uses the standard iPhone share sheet: it prepares your score card, a short message and the App Store link, and nothing is sent until you pick where to send it. We receive nothing.

Share and Ratings

If you tap Share, the game draws a picture of your score on your phone and opens the standard iOS share sheet with that picture and the App Store link, and you choose where it goes. That happens only when you tap, and it happens in Apple's apps, under Apple's privacy policy. Now and then, after you have played for a while and set a good new best, the game may ask Apple's own system to show its standard rating prompt. Apple decides whether to show it and limits how often. The game sends nothing anywhere to do this.

Apple and the App Store

Apple knows you downloaded the game, and whether you bought the optional full-game unlock, the way it knows about every purchase. We do not receive your name, your email or your purchase details from Apple.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, there is nothing about children to collect either.

If This Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top.
