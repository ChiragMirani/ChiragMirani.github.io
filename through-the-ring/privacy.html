Privacy

Play without sharing gameplay analytics. Optional data sharing helps improve Flappy Tappy.

Updated November 5, 2026. The optional analytics and cosmetic purchases below apply to version 2.5.3 and later. Earlier versions may have fewer features.

Your choice about gameplay data

After gameplay, we ask whether you want to share gameplay and performance data. Sharing is off until you agree. You can change your choice in Settings. Declining does not block play, and actions before agreement are not replayed into analytics. Turning sharing off stops new analytics and crash-report collection; it does not automatically delete reports already sent.

What optional analytics includes

We use Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config to understand button choices, screens, Solo and Duel results, retries, difficulty, time spent, reminders, experiment groups, shop selections and purchase outcomes. Data can include an installation identifier, app and device details, general location derived by the service, crash reports and performance diagnostics. The installation identifier is pseudonymous: it is not your name, but it can connect events from the same app installation.

When sharing is enabled, the app also asks Apple whether an Apple Ads ad led to the download. Available campaign information helps us compare paid and organic gameplay. We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), enable advertising-personalization signals, record your screen, collect message contents, or send Game Center player IDs to analytics. Game Center sign-in status may be recorded as yes or no.

Service providers and storage

Optional reports are processed by Google Firebase and Google Analytics. We export analytics into Google BigQuery for retention and experiment reports; raw Sandbox export tables currently expire after 60 days. That is not a promise that every Google report or backup is deleted after 60 days. Google services may process data in the United States and other locations. Firebase also supplies configuration and connection-time metadata when initialized. See Firebase privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

Scores, points and your collection

Scores, available earned points, ball ownership, avatar choice, settings, reminder preferences and saved runs are stored on your device. Spending points in the shop does not reduce your lifetime leaderboard score. The app does not require a separate Flappy Tappy account. Deleting the app removes its local save; Apple purchase entitlements and submitted Game Center scores are managed separately by Apple.

Game Center and friends

The app initializes Game Center at launch. Guest play remains available. Apple manages Game Center authentication, leaderboards and friend play. Other players may see the nickname, avatar and scores you share through Game Center under Apple's controls. Friend challenges and sharing use Apple's services or the destination you choose. Game Center identifiers and message contents are not included in our gameplay analytics.

Purchases

Apple processes optional purchases, including the existing full-game unlock and cosmetic balls. Ball purchases are non-consumable: each unlocks the named cosmetic without changing flight physics. Prices are displayed by Apple's StoreKit in your storefront currency. We receive verified product and transaction information needed to deliver and restore ownership, not payment card details. If analytics sharing is enabled, reports can include the product, purchase result, amount, currency and storefront. Restore and refunds are distinguished from new purchases. Use Restore in Shop for balls and Settings → Full Game for the full-game unlock.

Reminders

Reminders require your notification choice and are scheduled locally by iOS. Daily comeback reminders are limited to one a day and are skipped when the app records gameplay that day. You can turn them off in the game or in iOS Settings. Delivery timing depends on iOS and device settings.

Children and controls

The game does not ask for a child's name, email or date of birth. Optional data sharing can remain off. Apple Screen Time, Ask to Buy and notification controls apply. We do not use the gameplay data for personalized advertising.

Website visits

This support website uses GoatCounter to measure aggregate page visits. Website measurement is separate from the game's optional Firebase choice. See GoatCounter's privacy information.

Questions and requests

Email submartingaleoptions+throughthering@gmail.com about privacy or data requests. Because gameplay reports use an installation identifier rather than a named account, we may need information to locate the relevant data. Do not email passwords or payment details.
