A One-Tap Timing and Reflex Game for iPhone

Through the Ring PRO is a one-tap timing game for iPhone and iPad: you tap at the right moment to fly a ball through each ring, and the speed rises as your score grows. It tests reflexes and rhythm, not a millisecond reaction score, and it costs $2.99 once with no ads and no in-app purchases.

What It Asks of You

  • Timing: each tap is a jump, and gravity pulls you back. Passing a ring means judging the arc, not just reacting.
  • Precision: center passes score more, and a perfect streak multiplies your points.
  • Quick decisions: walls close if you miss the ring, and spikes line the floor and ceiling.
  • Rising speed: it starts slow, then picks up as you score.

Ways to Practice

Slow mode

20 percent slower, with its own best score. Good for beginners and younger players.

Practice

Unlimited retries to learn the timing, with no score pressure.

Today's leaderboard

A fresh board every day on Game Center, so there is always a new score to chase.

Honest Limits

  • It does not report a reaction time in milliseconds, and it is not a medical or training tool.
  • It does not claim to improve your reflexes outside the game.
  • One player only, iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later), portrait.

Questions People Ask

Is Through the Ring PRO a reaction time test?

No. It does not measure your reaction time in milliseconds. It is a timing game: you judge when to tap so the ball flies through each ring, and the game gets faster as you score.

Does it get harder?

Yes. It starts slow, then picks up as you score. Power Flight runs about 35 percent faster for its three guided rings.

Is there an easier way to start?

Yes. Slow mode makes the game 20 percent slower and keeps its own best score, and Practice gives you unlimited retries.

How long is a run?

As long as you last. You start with three lives, and the course never ends, so a run can be a few seconds or much longer.

Can I compare with other people?

Yes, if you use Game Center. Normal Play runs count on today's leaderboard and the all-time leaderboard, and the game shows your place after each run.
