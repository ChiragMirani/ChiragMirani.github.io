Flappy Tappy: a one-tap timing game for quick breaks

Flappy Tappy is a casual timing game: tap to lift the ball, release to fall and aim through rings. It does not measure reaction time in milliseconds or claim to improve medical or cognitive outcomes.

What makes a good run?

Judge the arc of each tap and aim for center passes. Perfect streaks multiply points. Solo lasts as long as your three lives allow; live duels last up to 30 seconds.

Play at your own pace

Start with free Solo or the browser preview, then try live duels in the app. Solo works offline. Multiplayer needs a connection.
