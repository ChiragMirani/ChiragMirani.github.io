An Offline iPhone Game with No Wi-Fi and No Ads

Through the Ring PRO is a one-tap arcade game for iPhone and iPad that plays fully offline: no Wi-Fi, no cellular data and no ads. It is free to try, with one optional one-time unlock and no subscription, and it works the same on a plane, on the subway or anywhere with no signal.

Why It Works Well Offline

  • Nothing to download during play. The course, sounds and music are all inside the app.
  • No account and no sign-in to get past before you can play.
  • No ads, so there is nothing waiting to load between runs.
  • Your run is saved. If the cabin lights come on or a message arrives, you can continue later.
  • One tap, short runs. Easy to play in a cramped seat with one thumb.

Before Your Flight

  1. Install it while you are online. The App Store needs a connection.
  2. Open it once and choose your sound and vibration settings. If you want unlimited runs, buy or restore the full-game unlock now, while you are online.
  3. Test it: turn on airplane mode, close the game, open it and play a round.
  4. Bring headphones if you want the music, or turn sound off in Settings.

What Needs a Connection

Only the optional extras: Game Center leaderboards, your place on today's leaderboard, and friend challenges. Buying or restoring the full-game unlock also needs a connection, because it goes through the App Store. The game itself plays exactly the same without them.

Honest Limits

  • It is a quick arcade game, not a long game to fill a 10-hour flight on its own.
  • It is free to try with 3 free runs, at most one per day. Unlimited play needs a one-time unlock, $2.99 in the US (prices vary by country).
  • iPhone and iPad only, iOS 17 or later, portrait.

Questions People Ask

Does Through the Ring PRO work in airplane mode?

Yes. The whole game plays with no internet connection, in airplane mode, with no Wi-Fi and no cellular data. Only the optional Game Center leaderboards and challenges need a connection.

Do I need to open it online first?

No sign-in or download inside the game is needed. Just install it from the App Store before you lose your connection, because the App Store itself needs the internet.

Will it show ads when I am offline?

No. It never shows ads, online or offline.

How do I test whether any game works offline?

Before a trip, turn on airplane mode, turn off Wi-Fi, force-close the game and open it again. If it starts and plays a full round, it works offline.

Does an offline game use battery?

Every game uses battery, and fast-moving games use more than reading. Lowering screen brightness helps. Through the Ring PRO lets you turn off music, sound and vibration separately in Settings.
