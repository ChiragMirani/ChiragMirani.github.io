Flappy Tappy offline: free Solo without Wi-Fi or ads

Flappy Tappy Solo works offline on iPhone and iPad, with endless free play and no ads. Live friend duels, matchmaking and FaceTime play need an internet connection.

Before a trip

  1. Download the app while connected.
  2. Open Solo and choose your sound settings.
  3. Turn on airplane mode, turn off Wi-Fi and play a complete round to test your device.

What needs a connection?

The App Store, buying or restoring purchases, online duels and Game Center services need a connection. Downloading the app first lets you use Solo when you have no signal.
