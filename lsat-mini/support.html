Common Questions

How do I unlock all the questions? Open Settings, then Full Access, then Unlock Everything. It is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store, with no subscription.

I bought it before. How do I get it back? Open Settings, then Full Access, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple account you bought with.

How do I turn reminders on or off? Settings, then Daily Reminder. If notifications are off, turn them on for LSAT Mini in the iOS Settings app.

How do I move my progress to a new phone? In Settings, under Your Data, export your progress and save the file. Then use Import Backup on the new device.

How do I start over? In Settings, under Your Data, choose Reset Progress. This removes saved tests and preferences from your device.

Do the practice questions predict my LSAT score? No. They are original practice material for short study sessions.
