MACRO VIEW FIRST, TIMING SECOND

Bullish on bonds. How much to add now?

Suppose your portfolio has 6 years of duration and your macro view supports a target of 7. Set the target and risk budget first; use the weekly evidence to review the entry.

01 / WAIT

The reading leans against you.

Hold at 6 years while you examine the opposing inputs. A weekly disagreement can change the entry without overturning the macro thesis.

02 / ADD

The evidence supports your view.

Consider moving toward the planned 7-year target if the portfolio conditions still fit. The checklist does not set the amount.

03 / BUILD

You prefer to stage the position.

With the same supportive signal, add 0.25 years to reach 6.25, then reassess before the next step.

Illustrative decisions, not a tested allocation rule. The model itself uses fixed +1, 0 or -1 positions.

QUICK ANSWERS

Using the Treasury checklist

What does the Treasury signal help me decide?

It is an additional timing check after you form a macro view: review whether to add duration now, wait, or build the planned position gradually.

Is the position scaled by the score?

No. The model outputs +1, 0 or -1. The score shows the balance of votes; the regression probability is uncalibrated. Portfolio size comes from your risk budget and implementation plan.

Can I use the page on Monday or another day?

Yes. You can check the latest published data on any day. The tested signal remains Friday to Friday; a Monday visit does not create a Monday-to-Monday forecast.

How are results measured?

The score is minus the position times the next weekly change in the 10-year Treasury yield, in basis points. Flat weeks score zero. It is a yield-direction measure, not a bond or ETF return.

Were these historical results selected after seeing the data?

Yes. Quarterly refits and the 30-year TIPS auction addition were selected after reviewing the later results. The preprint reports the full searches and benchmarks. Prospective forecasts are recorded separately.

What happens when data are late?

The page keeps the last complete reading and its original reference dates. It does not turn missing data into a neutral vote or backdate a new forecast. A new issue needs complete fresh inputs in the issue window.

Where are the paper and reproducible code?

The preprint page links the PDF, readable paper, full code package and historical result tables. The weekly tracker supplies JSON and CSV downloads.
