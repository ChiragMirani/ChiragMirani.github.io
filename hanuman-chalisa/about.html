About

The complete Hanuman Chalisa, presented one verse at a time with Devanagari, IAST transliteration, and a clear English meaning. Tap "Next verse" to move through the opening dohas, the forty chaupais, and the closing doha in order. Your position is saved in your browser.

Text

The Hanuman Chalisa is a devotional hymn attributed to Goswami Tulsidas. It is traditionally composed of two opening dohas, forty chaupais, and one closing doha. The Devanagari and transliteration in this app were checked against YogaWiki's Hanuman Chalisa text.

Translation

The English meanings are intentionally plain and compact, written for a verse-by-verse reader rather than as a scholarly commentary. The original hymn is old enough to be public domain.

Install on your home screen

On iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap the Share button, then tap "Add to Home Screen." On Android, use Chrome's "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" option. After the first visit, the app can load offline.
