Privacy

Flappy Tappy has no ads and never sells your data. Here is exactly what the Android game uses, and why.

Last updated Saturday, October 31, 2026 · Applies to Flappy Tappy: Fly Solo or Duel on Google Play (com.flappytappy). The iPhone and iPad version has its own page.

In Short

No ads. No selling or sharing of data for advertising by us. No accounts to make: you never give us your email or real name. Scores, settings and your saved friends live on your phone.

What Stays on Your Phone

Your best scores, total points, rank, settings, chosen picture, free-duel count, saved friends and your head-to-head records, and whether the full game is unlocked. They are stored in the app on your device. Uninstall the game and they are gone.

Game Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which parts of the game people enjoy and to measure whether our advertising works, the game sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example that the game was opened, a round or duel finished, a button was tapped, how long a screen was open, and which screens were visited. These events come with a random app-instance identifier, your device model, Android version, language, app version and an approximate country or region worked out from your network address. They do not include your name, email, contacts or precise location. If you came from one of our ads or a friend's link, the game also records which campaign or link brought you (through Google Play's install referrer).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks first, and analytics and advertising measurement stay off unless you agree. Google keeps this data for 14 months.

Play a Friend (Live Duels and Invitations)

Live duels with friends run through Google Firebase. When you first play a friend, the game signs in anonymously: Firebase gives this install a random identifier, with no email, phone number or password. To connect two phones, the game writes a short-lived duel room containing the five-letter code, the name you show (your Google Play Games name or one you type) and your picture choice, and the two phones exchange game moves through it. Rooms are deleted when the duel ends or the invitation runs out (at most a few minutes).

If you turn on notifications, the game stores this phone's notification token with your anonymous identifier so a friend can send you a duel invitation. Our server reads it only to deliver that invitation. Turning notifications off removes it.

Sharing a link or a challenge uses Android's share menu. You choose the app and the person; nothing is sent until you do.

Google Play Games (Leaderboards)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Solo totals go to Google Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see your Play Games name, picture and score according to your Play Games settings and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases and Reviews

The optional one-time unlock is sold by Google Play. Google handles payment; we never see your card or payment details, only that the game is unlocked. Now and then, after a good moment, the game may ask Google Play to show its standard review prompt; Google decides whether to show it.

Who Processes This Data

Google (Firebase, Google Analytics, Google Play, Google Play Games, Firebase Cloud Messaging) processes this data for us under its terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Data is encrypted in transit.

Children

Flappy Tappy is made for teenagers and adults (13 and over). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used Play a Friend, email us and we will delete it.

Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

You can turn off notifications in the game or in Android settings, decline analytics where asked, sign out of Google Play Games, and reset your advertising identifier in Android settings. To delete the analytics and friend-duel data linked to your install, email us from the game's support address below with the words "delete my data"; we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your phone.

If This Changes

If the game ever collects something new, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top.
