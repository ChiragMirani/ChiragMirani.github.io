Privacy

MCAT Prep: Mini Practice Tests does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What Stays on Your Device

Your answers, scores, streaks, badges, marked questions, saved practice and settings are stored only on your iPhone. We never see them. Deleting the app, or using Reset Progress in Settings, removes them. Because we hold no copy, we cannot retrieve or delete your data for you.

Reminders

Daily reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules and shows itself. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Optional Tips

The app can offer optional tips as Apple in-app purchases. Apple processes the payment under Apple's terms. We never see your card or payment details.

Game Center

The Future Doc League uses Apple's Game Center, and only if you tap to join. If you join and share a score, your Game Center name, rank and that score are visible to other players and held by Apple under Apple's terms. You can reset your Game Center score in iOS Settings. If you never join, nothing is sent.

When You Share

Result cards and progress backups leave your device only when you tap Share or Export and choose where they go.

Children

We collect nothing, so we hold no data on anyone of any age.

If This Changes

If we ever add anything that collects data, we will say so here before it ships. Last updated Saturday, October 17, 2026.
