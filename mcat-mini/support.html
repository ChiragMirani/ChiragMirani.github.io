Common Questions

How do I mark a question? Tap the flag on the question. Marked questions appear in the Review tab.

How do I change appearance? Settings, then Appearance: System (follows your iPhone), Ocean Blue or Dark.

How do I turn reminders on or off? Settings, then Daily Reminder. If notifications are off, turn them on for MCAT Mini in iOS Settings.

How do I move my progress to a new phone? Settings, then Progress, then Export Progress. Save the file, then use Import Backup on the new phone.

Do the practice questions predict my MCAT score? No. They are original practice material for study breaks.
