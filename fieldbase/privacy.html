OGI (Off Grid Intelligence) Privacy

OGI (Off Grid Intelligence) does not collect any data about you, and we receive nothing from the app. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. OGI has no server of its own. OGI was formerly called Fieldbase.

Updated Tuesday, November 24, 2026.

What Stays on Your Device

Your chats, notes, saved places and paths, imported documents and Portable Document Format (PDF) files, photos you take or import, voice recordings and their transcripts, saved maps and forecasts, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes this data. Because we hold no copy, we cannot retrieve or delete it for you.

The AI Assistant

The artificial intelligence (AI) assistant runs entirely on your iPhone. It uses Apple's Foundation Models framework, which runs on the device, and a small language model that also runs on the device. Your questions, its answers and your chats are not sent to any AI company.

Permissions

Camera: used only when you choose to take a photo. The photo is examined on the phone.

Microphone and speech recognition: used to record a short voice message and transcribe it on the phone only. If no offline language is available, the recording is not sent anywhere to be transcribed.

Location while using the app: used to show your coordinates and direction, save places and paths, and prepare nearby water, places and maps. Your location leaves the phone only in the cases listed below.

What Leaves Your Device, and When

All of the following happen only while you are connected to the internet and the app is open. None of them include your name, an account or an advertising identifier.

Online answers (off by default). OGI answers on the phone unless you allow online answers. The first time an answer would use the internet, OGI asks your permission first. Nothing is sent unless you tap Allow. If you allow it, OGI sends the text of that one question to Microsoft Bing search and reads up to 3 of the websites it returns. Coordinates, email addresses, web addresses and long numbers are removed from the question first. Your location, chat history, notes and documents are never sent. Urgent safety questions are always answered offline, from saved guidance.

Nearby water, places and paths. When you ask for nearby water while online, turn on area preparation in the OGI menu (off until you turn it on), or save walking paths for a trip, OGI sends the coordinates of the area around you (within about 5 kilometres, or 3 miles), or of the area between your start and destination, to OpenStreetMap data services (overpass-api.de, and api.openstreetmap.org as a backup).

Weather. With area preparation turned on and Wi-Fi connected, OGI sends your position rounded to about 1 kilometre to the United States National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) to save a local forecast.

State map suggestion. When you are online and have allowed location, OGI asks Apple's location service which state you are in (no more than about every five minutes, or sooner if you move), so it can suggest the right offline map. Apple handles this under Apple's terms.

Downloads you choose. Offline maps you pick are downloaded from the map tile host, bundle.tiles.coalition.space. The request names the map you chose, not your coordinates. Reference PDF files you pick are downloaded from their original publishers, such as the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), Ready.gov and the National Park Service.

As with any internet request, these services can see your device's internet protocol (IP) address. OGI sends us nothing.

Third Parties

Each service OGI contacts receives only what is described above. These are Microsoft Bing, OpenStreetMap services, the United States National Weather Service, Apple, the map tile host and the PDF publishers. Each handles requests under its own privacy policy. OGI and its developer hold no copy of any of your data.

How to Turn Off Online Answers

You can withdraw your permission at any time. Open the OGI menu and turn off the Online answers switch. This revokes the permission, and OGI stops sending questions to Microsoft Bing. You can turn area preparation off in the same menu.

How Long Data Is Kept, and How to Delete It

Your data stays on your iPhone for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app removes it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete.

When You Share

Places, coordinates, path files and extracted document text leave your device only when you tap Share and choose where they go. Path files include your precise locations and times.

Reminders

Readiness reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules and shows itself. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Safety

OGI gives general information. It is not a substitute for emergency services, medical care or local authorities, and it does not contact rescuers for you. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the United States).

Children

We collect nothing, so we hold no data on anyone of any age.

This Website

This website (not the app) counts page visits with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies or identify you.

If This Changes

If we ever add anything that collects data, we will say so here before it ships. This policy was updated and is effective Tuesday, November 24, 2026. It replaces the version effective Wednesday, November 4, 2026.
