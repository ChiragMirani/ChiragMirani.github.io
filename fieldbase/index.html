Fieldbase

Help that keeps working when the signal does not. Fieldbase is an offline survival companion for iPhone. Ask a question in plain words and it answers from guides and reference documents saved on the phone, with an assistant that runs on the device itself.

What It Does

Saved guidance for outages, storms, floods, first aid, water, shelter and more, with links back to the source documents. Field tools such as a compass, flashlight, Morse code and calculators. Offline maps you choose to download, saved places and your coordinates. Your own notes and documents, searchable on the phone. Voice messages are transcribed on the device.

When You Are Online

If you are connected, Fieldbase can look up current information, save a local weather forecast, and prepare nearby water, places and maps ahead of time. Each of these is described on the privacy page, and you can turn them off.

Good to Know

Fieldbase is in development and coming to the App Store. It is not available to download yet. Its guidance is general information and is not a substitute for emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the United States) first.
