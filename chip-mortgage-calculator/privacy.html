Privacy

Your calculator inputs and plans are not collected by us. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no account.

What Stays on Your Device

Your plans and the numbers you type are stored on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app removes its local data; exported files and device backups you keep may still contain copies. Manage those separately.

Public Mortgage-Rate Data

The first time you open the app, it asks the St. Louis Fed's FRED service (fred.stlouisfed.org) for the latest Freddie Mac 30-year average mortgage rate. This is public data. The app sends nothing about you with it. It sets no cookies and uses no account.

Like any web request, it reaches the Fed's server from your network address. The St. Louis Fed has its own privacy policy. If you are offline, the app uses a rate built into it instead.

Optional Purchases in Version 1.0.1

The planned update uses Apple's in-app purchase system for the permanent saving and export purchase. Apple handles payment, purchase history and refunds. The app verifies access through Apple's StoreKit and keeps an access flag on your device. We do not receive your card details or Apple Account password.

When You Share

Reports and plan files leave your device only when you tap Share and pick where they go.

Ratings

The app may show Apple's standard prompt to rate it. Apple runs that prompt. We get no data from it.

Children

We collect nothing, so we hold no data on anyone of any age.

When You Contact Support

If you email support, we receive the message and attachments you choose to send so we can respond. Do not include account numbers or unrelated personal financial information. Contact the address below with questions about a support message you sent.

If This Changes

If we ever add anything that collects data, we will say so here before it ships. Last updated October 21, 2026.
