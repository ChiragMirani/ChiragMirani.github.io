Does it support Spanish?

Yes. The app supports English, Mexican Spanish and Spain Spanish. It calculates US fixed-rate mortgages in US dollars; it is not a calculator for local Mexican or Spanish mortgage products.

What does the monthly payment include?

Principal and interest, property tax, insurance, applicable PMI, HOA dues, upkeep, utilities and any extra principal payment you enter.

Can I restore my purchase?

Yes. Open App Options, choose Full Calculator and tap Restore Purchase. Customers who bought the original paid app retain full access.
