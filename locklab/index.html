Watch. Learn. Practice. Celebrate.

Watch every turn, follow guided steps, then try your own three-number combination. Celebrate your success with Locky.

All practice features are free. No ads, accounts or subscriptions. Optional tips support the app and unlock no additional features.

How it works

  1. Start at 0. Turn right three full turns to reset.
  2. Keep turning right to your first number.
  3. Turn left. Pass your second number once, then stop on it the second time.
  4. Turn right to your third number.
  5. Pull up to open.

This is a simulator. Check your physical lock’s own instructions; different models can work differently.
