How it works

  1. Start at 0. Turn right three full turns to reset.
  2. Keep turning right to your first number.
  3. Turn left. Pass your second number once, then stop on it the second time.
  4. Turn right to your third number.
  5. Pull up to open.

This is a simulator. Check your physical lock’s own instructions; different models can work differently.

Questions people ask

How can my child practice a school locker combination lock before middle school?

Practice Combination Lock lets a child rehearse a three-number locker lock on an iPhone or iPad before the first day of school. Watch mode shows every turn, Learn mode guides each step, and Practice mode lets them try a combination of their own on a 0–39 dial, as many times as they like.

Is there a free app to practice opening a combination lock?

Yes. Practice Combination Lock is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and every practice feature is included at no cost. Optional one-time tips support the developer but unlock nothing extra. There are no ads, no subscriptions, no account and no tracking.

How do you open a three-number combination lock?

On a typical school locker lock, turn the dial right three full turns to reset, then keep turning right and stop on your first number. Turn left, pass your second number once and stop on it the second time. Turn right to your third number, then pull up. Check your own lock’s instructions.

Does Practice Combination Lock work offline?

Yes. The app works without an internet connection, so a child can practice at home, in the car or anywhere else once it is installed. It needs no account or sign-in, shows no ads and does not track you, so there is nothing to set up before the first try.

Does Practice Combination Lock have ads or in-app purchases?

There are no ads and no subscriptions. The only in-app purchases are optional one-time tips, which support the app and unlock no additional features, so someone who never tips gets exactly the same app. Every Watch, Learn and Practice feature is free from the first launch.

Which devices and which kind of lock does it support?

It runs on iPhone and iPad. It simulates a three-number rotary combination lock with a 0–39 dial, the right-left-right style used on many school lockers. Because it is a simulator, check your physical lock’s own instructions too, since different lock models can work differently.
