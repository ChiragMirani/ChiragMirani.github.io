NEET Mini

Four questions. One quick study break. Each mini-test has one question from each of Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology, or four from a subject you pick. After every answer you see right away whether you were correct and read the explanation.

What You Get

Mixed and subject practice, review of missed and marked questions, streaks, badges and a daily goal. Free, with no account and no ads.

Good to Know

These are original practice questions. Mini-tests do not predict an NEET score. Questions, explanations and menus are in English.
