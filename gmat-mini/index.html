Questions and Answers

What is GMAT Mini?

GMAT Mini is an iPhone and iPad app for GMAT practice in short sessions. Each mini quiz has four GMAT-style questions, one from each of Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning and Data Insights, plus one more that rotates between them.

How many questions are in a mini quiz?

Four. A mini quiz takes a few minutes, and every answer comes with a step-by-step explanation.

Is there a GMAT mini quiz app for iPhone?

Yes. GMAT Mini is built for iPhone and iPad, and it is a mini quiz app: you answer four GMAT-style questions at a time and read an explanation after each one.

Which GMAT sections does it cover?

The three sections of the GMAT Focus Edition: Quantitative Reasoning (Problem Solving), Verbal Reasoning (Critical Reasoning and Reading Comprehension) and Data Insights (Data Sufficiency, Table Analysis, Graphics Interpretation, Two-Part Analysis and Multi-Source Reasoning).

Can I practice one section at a time?

Yes. Besides the mixed mini quiz, you can choose a single subject (Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning or Data Insights) and get a mini quiz of four questions from it.

Can I retry the questions I missed?

Yes. The Review tab keeps the questions you missed or marked, and you can take a mini quiz made from the questions you missed.

Is there a timer?

A countdown timer is hidden by default. You can show it with the Show Timer switch in Settings.

Is GMAT Mini free?

Your first mini quiz is free. One purchase unlocks all 3,000 original practice questions. Pay once, no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Your practice history stays on your device.

Will my score in the app predict my GMAT score?

No. Mini quizzes add to your main preparation and do not predict a GMAT score.

Is GMAT Mini official?

No. It is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Graduate Management Admission Council. Questions are original practice material.

Which languages does the app support?

The menus and buttons are in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean and Italian. The questions and explanations are in English, as on the GMAT itself.
