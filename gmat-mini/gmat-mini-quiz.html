GMAT Mini Quiz: How a Four-Question Daily Habit Works

A GMAT mini quiz in GMAT Mini is four questions and takes a few minutes. This page explains what happens in one, what the app offers around it, and a simple routine you can adapt. It describes the app as it works today; the mini quiz is one part of preparation and sits beside longer practice.

What Happens in One Mini Quiz

  1. You get four questions. One is from Quantitative Reasoning, one from Verbal Reasoning, one from Data Insights, and a fourth from a section that rotates, so no section is left out for long.
  2. You answer each one. You pick a choice, and the app shows right away whether it was correct.
  3. You read the explanation. Every answer comes with a step-by-step explanation: the idea, the steps and why the other choices fail.
  4. You see your results. The first mini quiz is free. After that, one purchase unlocks all 3,000 questions, with no subscription.

A countdown timer is hidden by default. Turn it on with the Show Timer switch in Settings if you want to practice under time pressure. If you close the app in the middle of a mini quiz, it resumes on the same question with your answers kept.

Other Ways to Practice

  • One section only: choose Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning or Data Insights and take a mini quiz of four questions from it.
  • Retry your misses: the Review tab keeps questions you missed or marked, and you can take a mini quiz made from them.
  • Progress: the Progress tab shows how your practice is going over time.
  • Reminder: an optional daily reminder, set in Settings.

A Simple Daily Routine

These are suggestions for building the habit, not claims about results.

  1. Pick a fixed moment, such as after breakfast or on the commute, and take one mini quiz.
  2. Spend more time on the explanations than on the answering. A wrong answer you understand is useful practice.
  3. Once a week, take a mini quiz from your missed questions, and one from your weakest section.
  4. Keep full-length, timed practice for your main study sessions. The real sections have 20 to 23 questions each, far more than a mini quiz.

What GMAT Mini Does Not Do

Scores in the app do not predict your GMAT score. The questions are original practice material, not real exam questions, and the app is not affiliated with the test maker. There is no account, no ads and no tracking, and your practice history stays on your device. See the privacy page.

Questions and Answers

How long does a GMAT mini quiz take?

A few minutes. A mini quiz is four questions, so it fits a commute or a lunch break.

What is in one mini quiz?

Four GMAT-style questions: one from Quantitative Reasoning, one from Verbal Reasoning, one from Data Insights and a fourth from a section that rotates.

Can I get a reminder?

Yes. An optional daily reminder is in Settings. It is a local notification that your device schedules itself, and nothing about it is sent to us.

Can a mini quiz replace full-length practice?

No. Mini quizzes add to your main preparation. They are short, and they do not predict a GMAT score.
