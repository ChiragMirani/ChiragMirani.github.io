Privacy

GMAT Mini does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What Stays on Your Device

Your answers, scores, marked questions, practice history and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We never see them. Deleting the app, or using Reset Progress in Settings, removes them. Because we hold no copy, we cannot retrieve or delete your data for you.

Reminders

Daily reminders are local notifications that your device schedules and shows itself. Nothing about them is sent to us.

The Purchase

The app offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks all questions. Apple processes the payment under Apple's terms. We never see your card or payment details.

When You Share

Result cards and progress backups leave your device only when you tap Share or Export and choose where they go. That destination's privacy terms apply.

Children

We collect nothing, so we hold no data on anyone of any age.

If This Changes

If we ever add anything that collects data, we will say so here before it ships. Last updated Tuesday, October 20, 2026.
