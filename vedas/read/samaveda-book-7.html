Samaveda Second Part - Book 1., 1. Soma Pavamana (Chapter 1.) — 1. Soma Pavamana (Chapter 1.)

1. Sing forth to Indu, O ye men, to him who now is purified, Fain to pay worship to the Gods!

2. Together with thy pleasant juice the Atharvans have commingled. milk. Divine, God-loving, for the God.

3. Bring health to cattle with thy flow, health to the people, health, to steeds, Health, O thou King, to growing plants!
