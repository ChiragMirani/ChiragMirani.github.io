Which is best for your child?

For English word practice with no purchase, start with VocabDeck. For offline subject vocabulary across all three subjects, try Vocabula. If a browser is easier for your family, use VocabDeck on the web.

A short study routine

  1. Pick the learner’s grade and one subject.
  2. Read a term and try to explain it before revealing the definition.
  3. Use the word in an example from class or everyday life.
  4. Revisit unfamiliar words in another short session.

These are vocabulary tools, not complete courses or a guarantee of test results. Grade labels follow the US school system; all cards and definitions are in English.
