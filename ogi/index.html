OGI: Off Grid Intelligence

Survival AI that keeps working when the signal does not. Ask a question in plain words and OGI answers from guides and books saved on your iPhone, with an AI that runs on the phone itself.

What It Does

First aid and survival guides with links back to their sources. Field tools such as a compass, flashlight, Morse code and calculators. Your state's offline map, saved places and the nearest pharmacies. Your own notes and documents, which the AI can answer from. Family chat over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi with no internet and no cell service.

Free and Private

OGI is free, with no account, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone. See the privacy page.

Good to Know

OGI gives general information and is not a substitute for emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the United States) first.
