Origin and Timeline

Off Grid Intelligence (OGI) was conceived, designed and built by Chirag Mirani in Suwanee, Georgia, USA.

This page records when the idea and its features were first written down and published. Each public entry can be checked in this website's public change history on GitHub, which timestamps every change. Times are United States Eastern.

Features as First Described

An AI that runs entirely on the iPhone and answers in plain words with no internet; step-by-step first aid and survival guides from public sources, with the source shown; an emergency page with Call 911 and Text 911; offline state maps with nearby hospitals, pharmacies, shelters and water; field tools (compass, where am I, way back, find water, flashlight and SOS signals, cardiopulmonary resuscitation metronome, daylight); your own documents that the AI can answer from; pack lists; photo and sound check; and messages between nearby phones with no signal.
