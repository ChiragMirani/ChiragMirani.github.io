Support

In an emergency, call 911 first (or your local emergency number). OGI gives general information and is not a substitute for emergency services. It does not contact rescuers for you.

Contact

Something wrong with a guide, an answer, a map or a tool? Email chirag_mirani+OGI@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing. Please do not send personal medical details or your location.

Common Questions

Does OGI work with no signal?
Yes. The AI chat, guides, included books, tools and any maps you have already downloaded all work in airplane mode. Your state's map downloads on Wi-Fi once you allow location. Download any extra books on Wi-Fi before you need them.

Is OGI really free?
Yes. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase, no ads and no account.

Which AI does OGI use?
Liquid AI's LFM3-Omni-2B model, built into the app and running on your iPhone. On eligible iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, OGI can also use Apple's on-device model. Neither sends your questions anywhere.

How do I message my family and friends with no signal?
Everyone needs OGI installed. Tap Invite family & friends on the Home screen and share the link; it opens a page where they can get OGI. Then, while you are together, open Family & friends chat, add each other and check that both phones show the same code. Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on. Range is roughly tens of metres between phones, more in the open, and other OGI phones nearby can pass messages along. Delivery is not guaranteed, so always call 911 in an emergency if you can.

Will OGI tell me if a plant or mushroom is safe to eat?
No. OGI never says a plant or mushroom is edible from a photo, because mistakes can kill. Photo check can describe what it sees and point you to the relevant guides.

Why won't the AI give me a medicine dose?
OGI never makes up doses. Where a checked guide or the medicine's label gives a dose, OGI shows that source word for word. Otherwise, it tells you to follow the label or a clinician's advice.

Can map labels tell me water is safe to drink?
No. Map labels do not confirm drinking safety, open facilities or safe routes. Treat water from any natural source before drinking.

How do I add my own guides and notes?
Open Guides, then My guides & notes, and add a note or a document. The AI can answer from them and links to the place in your document where it found the answer. They stay on your iPhone.

How do I control what uses the internet?
Open About & settings. Web search is off unless you turn on "Search the web when online". Area information is fetched only when you tap "Refresh now", set your home, or turn on "Keep my area ready".

Why did voice transcription not work?
Transcription runs only on the phone. If iOS has no offline speech language installed for your language, OGI cannot transcribe the recording.
