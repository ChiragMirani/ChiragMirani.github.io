Off Grid Intelligence (OGI)

Text family & friends with no signal

A free iPhone app. Chat over Bluetooth when there's no cell signal or Wi-Fi, and get offline first aid and survival help.

Free · No account · No tracking

  2. 2Open Family & friends chat and pair phones while you're together.
  3. 3Then text, send "I'm OK" or ask for help over Bluetooth, even with no signal.
