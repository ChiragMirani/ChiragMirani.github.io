Privacy policy: Series 65 Exam Practice Tests for Android

Last updated Thursday, November 19, 2026

No account or sign-in is needed. Your study answers, notes and progress stay on your device. The app shows no ads. It uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used, as described below; this never includes your study content.

What is stored, and where

The app keeps a record of your study: which cards you have seen, how you rated them, which you bookmarked, your practice test answers, and any problem you reported about a card. That record is a file inside the app's own storage on your device. The record itself is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it, so use Export in Settings first if you want to keep a copy.

Links, reports and sharing

Each card links to the public page its rule came from; tapping one opens that page in your browser, and that organization decides what it records. Reporting a card saves the report on your device; if you choose to send it, your mail app opens with the text already written so you can read it first or decide not to send it. Sharing the app passes a short description and a link, with nothing about your study. All of these are optional.

Buying the full deck

Google Play handles the purchase. We never see your name, your payment details or your Google account. The app receives a purchase record from Google Play (product, order identifier, date, purchase state, price and currency) so it can unlock the content. Restoring purchases checks your existing Google Play purchases.

Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service, to learn how people use the app so we can improve it, and to measure how well our own ads on Google work.

What is collected: the actions you take in the app, such as opening the app, seeing or skipping the welcome screen, opening the unlock screen, starting, buying or restoring a purchase, using an access code, starting, finishing or leaving a practice test (with its length, mode and score), opening a mistake review, finishing a flashcard sitting (how many cards and how many marked Got it), rating, bookmarking or reporting a card (that it happened, not which card or what you wrote), sharing the app, opening Settings, exporting progress, tapping Home, and the app asking for a rating. Along with these, Google Analytics records a random app instance identifier created for this installation, your device model, Android version, app version, language, and an approximate country or region worked out from your internet address (the address itself is not stored by Google Analytics). Purchase events include the price and currency of the unlock. We also note whether the full deck is unlocked and roughly how many tests you have finished (for example, 2 to 4).

What is not collected: your name, email address, Google account, payment details, the advertising identifier (the app does not request it), or any study content: no card or question text, your answers, notes, or what you wrote in a card report.

Retention: Google Analytics keeps this event data for up to 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically. Deleting the app removes its app instance identifier, so a reinstall starts a new, unlinked one.

How it is used: only to improve the app and to measure our own advertising, including letting Google Ads count installs and purchases that came from our ads. It is not sold, and it is not used to show you ads inside this app. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play install measurement (only if enabled)

A future version may measure our own advertising. If that is turned on, the app reads the install referrer from Google Play once and keeps it on your device, and new verified purchases and refunds are reported to our private reporting sheet hosted by Google with a hashed order identifier, day, amount, currency, app version and campaign details. We would never send your Google account, name, email address, payment details, advertising identifier, device identifier or study content, and this policy will be updated before it is enabled.

Children

This is a study aid for an adult professional licensing examination. It is not directed at children.
