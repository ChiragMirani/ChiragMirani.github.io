Privacy policy: Series 65 Exam Practice Tests for Android

Last updated Thursday, November 19, 2026

No account or sign-in is needed. Your study answers, notes and progress stay on your device. The app has no advertising and no analytics.

What is stored, and where

The app keeps a record of your study: which cards you have seen, how you rated them, which you bookmarked, your practice test answers, and any problem you reported about a card. That record is a file inside the app's own storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it, so use Export in Settings first if you want to keep a copy.

Links, reports and sharing

Each card links to the public page its rule came from; tapping one opens that page in your browser, and that organization decides what it records. Reporting a card saves the report on your device; if you choose to send it, your mail app opens with the text already written so you can read it first or decide not to send it. Sharing the app passes a short description and a link, with nothing about your study. All of these are optional.

Buying the full deck

Google Play handles the purchase. We never see your name, your payment details or your Google account. The app receives a purchase record from Google Play (product, order identifier, date, purchase state, price and currency) so it can unlock the content. Restoring purchases checks your existing Google Play purchases.

Google Play install measurement (only if enabled)

A future version may measure our own advertising. If that is turned on, the app reads the install referrer from Google Play once and keeps it on your device, and new verified purchases and refunds are reported to our private reporting sheet hosted by Google with a hashed order identifier, day, amount, currency, app version and campaign details. We would never send your Google account, name, email address, payment details, advertising identifier, device identifier or study content, and this policy will be updated before it is enabled.

Children

This is a study aid for an adult professional licensing examination. It is not directed at children.
