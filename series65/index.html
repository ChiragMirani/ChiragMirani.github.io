Common questions

How many questions are on the Series 65 exam?

140 questions: 130 scored questions plus 10 unscored pretest questions mixed in, so you can't tell which ones don't count. You have 180 minutes, about 77 seconds a question.

What score do you need to pass the Series 65?

You need at least 92 of the 130 scored questions right, which is about 71%. Results are shown on screen right after you finish.

What topics does the Series 65 cover?

Four sections, from the North American Securities Administrators Association (NASAA) outline that took effect on June 12, 2023: Economic Factors and Business Information (15%, 20 questions), Investment Vehicle Characteristics (25%, 32 questions), Client Investment Recommendations and Strategies (30%, 39 questions), and Laws, Regulations and Guidelines, Including Prohibition on Unethical Business Practices (30%, 39 questions).

Who writes and runs the Series 65 exam?

NASAA develops the exam and the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) administers it. The exam fee is $187, paid to FINRA. It is closed book.

How much does Series 65 Exam Practice Tests cost?

The download is free and includes one 10-question practice test and 10 flashcards. One $19.99 in-app payment (US price) unlocks all 1,211 questions and 1,143 flashcards for good. There is no subscription.

Do the practice tests match the real exam?

Every test, from 10 questions up to the full 140, uses the real exam's section mix (30% laws and regulations, 30% client recommendations, 25% investment vehicles, 15% economic factors), and the timer runs at the real pace of 77 seconds a question.

Does the app work offline, and what does it collect?

Everything is inside the app and works without internet. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Your answers stay on your device, and you can export them.

Will the app tell me if I'm ready to pass?

No. It does not give a pass probability or predict your result, because no study app can honestly do that. It shows what you answered, your score in each of the four sections, and the rules you missed.
